Privacy Policy

The short version: your day stays on your device. Last updated 2026.

What Luvli keeps, and where

Everything — your account, your plan, your focus history and your check-ins — is stored in your own browser, in localStorage under the key luvli.state.v1. It does not go to us. There is no Luvli server holding your day.

Your password

Luvli never stores your password. It keeps only a salted SHA-256 hash, computed in your browser with Web Crypto. Signing in with Google means Google holds that credential, never Luvli.

The device backup

A quiet IndexedDB mirror keeps a copy on the same device, so you can recover your data if something goes wrong. It never leaves your machine.

When data can leave your device

Only ever because you asked it to, and only to somewhere you chose:

  • Sync — off by default. If you enter an endpoint you control, a copy of your JSON is sent there.
  • The AI coach — off by default. If you switch it to a language model, the message you send (with a short summary of your day) is forwarded to that model so it can reply. Your model API key lives on your own serverless function, never in the browser.

In both cases, nothing is sent until you configure it yourself.

What Luvli does not do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No advertising, and no selling of data — ever.
  • No reading of your data by us. We cannot: it is not on our servers.

Distraction support

The study-app and distraction lists are preferences only. Luvli never inspects, closes or blocks other applications.

Your control

From Settings → Your data you can export everything as JSON, import a backup, or erase it all. Clearing your browser's storage for this site removes Luvli entirely.

Children

Luvli is not directed at children. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country, please use it with a parent or guardian.
