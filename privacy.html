Privacy Policy

The short version: your day is stored securely, and only you can ever see it. Last updated 2026.

What Luvli keeps, and where

Your account, your plan, your focus history, your study data and your check-ins are stored in a real database, protected by Row Level Security — a database-level rule that only lets your own signed-in account read or change your own rows. A working copy is also kept on your device (in your browser's storage) so Luvli stays fast and keeps working offline; it syncs back to your account the next time you're online.

Your password

Luvli never sees or stores your password in readable form. Signing up and signing in are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth), which keeps only a securely hashed version — never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, Google holds that credential instead; Luvli only receives confirmation of who you are.

Who can see your data

Only you, while signed in. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app's code, so no other account can ever read your data. We do not browse, read or export your data ourselves.

The AI coach

By default the coach runs entirely on your device — nothing you say to it leaves your browser. If the real AI coach is switched on, your message (with a short summary of your day) is sent to a secured endpoint that only a signed-in Luvli account can reach, which forwards it to a language model provider (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) to write a reply. That provider's own privacy policy applies to that message. Their API key is never exposed to your browser.

What Luvli does not do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No advertising, and no selling of data — ever.
  • No sharing your data with anyone else unless you ask us to.

Distraction support

The study-app and distraction lists are preferences only. Luvli never inspects, closes or blocks other applications.

Your control

From Settings → Your account you can rename yourself, sign out, or permanently delete your account — which erases your account and every piece of data tied to it, everywhere, immediately and irreversibly.

Children

Luvli is not directed at children. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country, please use it with a parent or guardian.
