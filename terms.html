Your plan, your focus history and your check-ins belong to you. Luvli does not sell them, and does not send them anywhere unless you explicitly configure a sync endpoint or the AI coach. You can export or erase everything at any time from Settings → Your data.

4. The AI coach

If you switch the coach to a language model, messages you send are forwarded to that model to produce a reply. The coach offers suggestions and encouragement — it is not professional, medical, legal or financial advice. Always use your own judgement, and turn to a qualified professional for anything serious.
